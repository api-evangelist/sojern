# Sojern
> Sojern is the leading AI-powered marketing platform built for hospitality, designed to boost growth and profitability for the travel industry. The Sojern marketing platform is a set of easy-to-use software and services that delivers unrivaled traveler insight, intelligent audiences, multichannel activation and optimization, and a connected guest experience—all in one place.

## LLM Summary
Sojern is the world’s #1 marketing platform for hospitality and travel. Powered by the Sojern Traveler Ecosystem™, the platform unifies real-time traveler intent data, AI-driven predictions, and multichannel media activation to help hotels, destinations, attractions, airlines, and more drive direct demand. With a global dataset, AI-powered automation and optimization, and deep travel expertise, Sojern enables travel marketers to reach the right traveler at the right moment across display, social, video, CTV, SEM, metasearch, email, and native channels.

Sojern's all-in-one marketing platform provides end-to-end capabilities—audiences, creative services, activation, optimization, measurement, and attribution—so travel brands can increase direct bookings, improve ROI, and grow long-term customer value.
